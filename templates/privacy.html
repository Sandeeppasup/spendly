Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, to record and summarize your expenses, and to show you spending breakdowns. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers we control and is retained for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorized access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third party services to operate, such as Google Fonts to load the typefaces used on this site. These providers may receive technical information like your IP address when your browser requests their resources. We do not share your expense data with them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
